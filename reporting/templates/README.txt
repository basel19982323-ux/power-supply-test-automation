Drop your company report templates here.

  company_report.xlsx   - an Excel template; fill locations are set in a
                          mapping.yaml (see ../mapping.example.yaml).
  company_deck.pptx      - a PowerPoint template; its theme/master/branding is
                          used as the base for the generated deck.

On the Report tab, tick "Use company template" and pick the file(s). Until then,
the app generates a clean built-in layout with the same data.
